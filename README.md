# OVERVIEW

This repository contains Big Query code using Google Analytics raw data tracking, analysing, and surfacing insights on Out-of-Stock (OOS)

# DASHBOARD

The data studio dashboard (https://datastudio.google.com/reporting/53cfbbc3-1b05-4e65-9a82-0577a19e4457) brings many of the insights to life.

## METHODOLOGY & STRATEGIC CONTEXT 

### OBJECTIVE
Standard e-commerce reporting tracks macro conversion rates and total site traffic but routinely obscures the hidden revenue leakages caused by inventory stockouts. When high-intent shoppers reach an out-of-stock (OOS) Product Detail Page (PDP), standard conversion metrics fail to isolate whether the user abandoned the session entirely or pivoted to purchase an alternative item.

The dashboard quantifies inventory friction in two core dimensions:
1. **Session-Level Funnel Impact & Revenue Leakage (Page 1):** Measures macro user behavior, identifying how out-of-stock PDP exposure impacts session outcomes (Abandoned vs. Recovered) and mapping these friction points directly back to paid/organic acquisition channels to prevent wasted ad spend.
2. **PDP-Level Demand & SKU Inventory Friction (Page 2):** Isolates shopper demand intensity at the product and category level, measuring view rates, repeat browsing friction (`Views per Session Index`), and inventory exposure across stock states (`instock`, `outofstock`, `onbackorder`).

### KEY BEHAVIORAL DEFINITIONS & JOURNEY TAXONOMY
To eliminate ambiguity across cross-functional teams, sessions and product views are classified using strict logical rules:

* **Session Outcome Categories (`session_type`):**
  * **Pure In-Stock PDP Session:** Sessions that viewed at least one PDP, where all viewed items were fully in-stock.
  * **Non-PDP Session:** Sessions that browsed the store (home page, category pages) but never reached a single PDP.
  * **OOS Abandoned (No Purchase):** High-intent sessions exposed to one or more out-of-stock PDPs that dropped off without making a purchase. Represents direct revenue leakage.
  * **OOS Recovered (Purchased):** High-intent sessions exposed to an out-of-stock PDP that successfully cross-sold or converted on an alternative in-stock product. Represents recovered opportunity.

* **Inventory Friction Indices:**
  * **OOS Exposure Rate (%):** The probability of a product-intent session encountering an out-of-stock page (`SUM(oos_pdp_sessions) / SUM(pdp_sessions)`).
  * **OOS Recovery Rate (%):** The effectiveness of site cross-selling and alternative recommendations when stockouts occur (`OOS Recovered Sessions / Total OOS PDP Sessions`).
  * **Views per Session Index:** Indicates repeat view behavior on product pages (`SUM(total_pdp_views) / SUM(pdp_sessions)`). High values on out-of-stock items signal elevated customer frustration and unfulfilled demand.

## KPI FRAMEWORK

The core KPIs tracked provide strategic insights across four critical business dimensions: **Revenue**, **Efficiency**, **Diagnostic**, and **Friction**.

| KPI Category | Metric Name | Metric Definition | Strategic Business Value | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Revenue** | **OOS Recovered Revenue (£)** | Revenue saved by customers who hit an OOS item but bought an alternative available SKU. | Quantifies total top-line financial value saved through automated on-site cross-sell recommendations (**Point 1**). | 
| **Efficiency** | **OOS Recovery Rate (%)** | Conversion efficiency of shoppers encountering an OOS item. | Measures front-end merchandising and UX effectiveness at redirecting unfulfilled intent into completed orders. | 
| **Diagnostic** | **OOS Revenue Share (%)** | Percentage of total site sales reliant on stockout cross-sell recoveries. | Diagnoses business risk exposure: indicates how dependent site revenue is on substitute products vs. primary inventory. | 
| **Friction** | **OOS Exposure Rate (%)** | Proportion of product-browsing visits exposed to unavailable stock. | Highlights customer experience friction and supply chain stock availability breakdowns. | 

### KEY KPI TAKEAWAYS

* **Revenue vs. Efficiency:** `OOS Recovered Revenue` measures the absolute monetary value retained, `OOS Recovery Rate (%)` gauges the conversion ratio of that traffic. A low recovery rate indicates weak substitute recommendation algorithms or pricing mismatches on PDP pages.
* **Diagnostic Risk:** If `OOS Revenue Share (%)` spikes too high, it indicates underlying inventory health issues where the store is forced to over-rely on fallback cross-sells rather than fulfilling primary product demand.
* **Friction Monitoring:** `OOS Exposure Rate (%)` serves as the primary health metric for supply chain performance—high exposure directly correlates with higher customer abandonment and net revenue leakage.

## CHANNEL GROUPING FIELD LOGIC

### DEFAULT CHANNEL GROUPING

```text
CASE
  WHEN REGEXP_MATCH(source_medium, '(?i)\\(direct\\) / \\(none\\)') THEN 'Direct'
  WHEN REGEXP_MATCH(source_medium, '(?i).* / (cpc|ppc|paidsearch)') THEN 'Paid Search'
  WHEN REGEXP_MATCH(source_medium, '(?i).* / (paidsocial|paid_social)') THEN 'Paid Social'
  WHEN REGEXP_MATCH(source_medium, '(?i).* / (paidvideo|paid_video)') THEN 'Paid Video'
  WHEN REGEXP_MATCH(source_medium, '(?i).* / (organicsocial|social)') THEN 'Organic Social'
  WHEN REGEXP_MATCH(source_medium, '(?i).* / organic') THEN 'Organic Search'
  WHEN REGEXP_MATCH(source_medium, '(?i).* / (email|newsletter)') THEN 'Email'
  WHEN REGEXP_MATCH(source_medium, '(?i).* / referral') THEN 'Referral'
  ELSE 'Unassigned / Other'
END
```
### PAID V NON PAID CHANNEL GROUPING

```text
CASE
  WHEN REGEXP_MATCH(source_medium, '(?i)\\(direct\\) / \\(none\\)') THEN 'Direct'
  WHEN REGEXP_MATCH(source_medium, '(?i).* / (cpc|ppc|paidsearch|paidsocial|paid_social|paidvideo|paid_video|display|cpm|banner)') THEN 'Paid Traffic'
  WHEN REGEXP_MATCH(source_medium, '(?i).* / (organic|organicsocial|social|email|newsletter|referral)') THEN 'Non-Paid Traffic'
  ELSE 'Unassigned / Other'
END
```

# OUT-OF-STOCK (OOS) DEMAND & GHOST DEMAND CALCULATOR

A financial and operational supply chain calculator connecting Google Analytics front-end e-commerce session telemetry with back-end inventory planning.

https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1oIqHRXCXIXxDzHKdiuGVGJUel8emKDrHZOlqBxhUtdQ/edit?usp=sharing

## OVERVIEW 

Traditional inventory management systems (ERP/WMS) record completed sales transactions. When a SKU goes out of stock, sales drop to zero—causing forecasting algorithms to treat the stockout period as zero demand.

The Google Sheets model bridges **Merchandising Recovery** and **Supply Chain Optimization** by taking out-of-stock session telemetry from Data Studio / BigQuery to:
1. **Quantify Net Revenue Leakage (£):** Calculate actual revenue lost after accounting for cross-sell recoveries on substitute products.
2. **Surface Uncaptured "Ghost Demand":** Convert unfulfilled PDP browsing velocity into an annualized demand baseline.
3. **Correct Economic Order Quantity (EOQ):** Prevent under-purchasing on future purchase orders by factoring ghost demand into reorder calculations.

**Methodology Note on Traffic Mixing & Channel Strategy:**
 
* **Traffic Channel Flexibility:** Column D (`Total OOS PDP Sessions`) and Column E (`Baseline In-Stock CVR`) are intentionally agnostic. For paid-led SKUs (e.g., 90% paid traffic), input total blended or paid sessions; for organic-led SKUs (e.g., 90% organic traffic), input organic sessions.
* **Ad-Pause Rule:** If paid media campaigns (e.g., Google Shopping, Meta Ads) are automatically paused upon stockout, using pure organic sessions provides an unforced baseline demand floor. If paid ads are scheduled to reactivate immediately upon restock, using total blended sessions ensures your Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) reflects full operational demand.

## Dual-Scenario Framework: Pre-OOS Velocity vs. Actual OOS Telemetry


When an e-commerce SKU hits zero inventory, automated ad rules (e.g., Google Shopping or Meta ad pauses) suppress incoming paid traffic. Relying solely on post-stockout web traffic creates a distorted picture of true product demand.

To solve this, the framework models each SKU across two distinct, purpose-built scenarios:

```
                     ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                     │              Dual-Scenario Demand Model                 │
                     └────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                                                  │
                ┌─────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────┐
                ▼                                                                   ▼
  1. Pre-OOS Velocity (Full Channel)                                 2. Actual OOS (Suppressed/Organic)
  ──────────────────────────────────                                 ──────────────────────────────────
  • Captures TOTAL unsuppressed demand                               • Captures UNFORCED organic demand
  • Inputs: Pre-OOS session run-rate                                 • Inputs: Live OOS traffic & 28% recovery
  • Recovery Rate: 0.00% (Gross Opportunity)                         • Recovery Rate: 28.00% (Telemetry)
  • Focus: Sizing Purchase Orders (310 EOQ)                          • Focus: Measuring Net Leakage (£3.9K)
```

### Scenario A: `Pre-OOS Velocity (Full Channel)` [Row 4 in Spreadsheet]

* **Primary Purpose:** **Supply Chain Reorder Sizing (EOQ) & Gross Opportunity Exposure**.
* **How It Works:**
  * **Session Input:** Extrapolates pre-stockout session velocity (combining paid search, paid social, organic, direct, and email) across the 14-day stockout window (e.g., 4,200 sessions).
  * **Recovery Rate Logic (0.00%):** Set to `0.00%` to model total unmitigated demand exposure. Because paid campaigns were paused during stockout, setting recovery to zero aligns **Gross Lost Revenue** directly with **Net Revenue Leakage (£12,495.00)**.
  * **Operational Value:** Procurement orders off the resulting **Annualized Ghost Demand (3,833 units)** to calculate an **Adjusted EOQ of 310 units**. This ensures that when restock arrives and paid marketing reactivates, inventory doesn't immediately sell out again.

### Scenario B: `Actual OOS (Suppressed/Organic)` [Row 5 in Spreadsheet]

* **Primary Purpose:** **Merchandising Performance & Net Financial Leakage**.
* **How It Works:**
  * **Session Input:** Uses live web telemetry captured by Looker Studio / GA4 while the item was out of stock (e.g., 1,822 organic/direct sessions).
  * **Recovery Rate Logic (28.00%):** Incorporates the live OOS recovery rate captured on-site (`28.00%`). It credits the site's recommendation engine for redirecting unfulfilled intent to substitute products (recovering £1,517.73).
  * **Operational Value:** Proves that actual net financial leakage was reduced to **£3,902.72**. It serves as a diagnostic metric for UX/merchandising teams to evaluate substitute page performance.

### Data Studio Telemetry Integration & Field Mapping Guide

To populate the orange input columns in your spreadsheet, map the three rows from your **Looker Studio Dashboard Table** (`Products Impacted by Stockouts: Recovery & Leakage Analysis`) directly into the respective spreadsheet scenario rows:

```

Line 3: Sunset St Pauls Graphic Tee | OOS Recovered (Purchased)  |    64 Sessions |  64 Purchasing |100% CVR | £5,440
```

#### Step-by-Step Scenario Input Mapping

#### 1. Scenario Row Assignment: `Row 4 - Pre-OOS Velocity (Full Channel)`
* **Target Spreadsheet Row:** **Row 4**
* **Session Input (`Total OOS PDP Sessions` - Column D):** Input pre-stockout run-rate volume (e.g., 4,200 sessions extrapolated from full-channel in-stock velocity).
* **`Baseline In-Stock CVR (%)` (Column E):** **`22.00%`** $\rightarrow$ Pulled from **Line 1** (`Pure In-Stock PDP Session` row $\rightarrow$ `PDP CVR (%)` column).
* **`OOS Recovery Rate (%)` (Column H):** **`0.00%`** $\rightarrow$ Hardcoded to 0% to isolate gross opportunity exposure and reorder sizing (EOQ).

#### 2. Scenario Row Assignment: `Row 5 - Actual OOS (Suppressed/Organic)`
* **Target Spreadsheet Row:** **Row 5**
* **Session Input (`Total OOS PDP Sessions` - Column D):** **`229 Sessions`** $\rightarrow$ Calculated by adding **Line 2** (`165 OOS Abandoned`) + **Line 3** (`64 OOS Recovered`).
* **`Baseline In-Stock CVR (%)` (Column E):** **`22.00%`** $\rightarrow$ Pulled from **Line 1** (`Pure In-Stock PDP Session` row $\rightarrow$ `PDP CVR (%)` column).
* **`OOS Recovery Rate (%)` (Column H):** **`28.00%`** $\rightarrow$ Calculated from telemetry: $\frac{\text{Line 3 Sessions (64)}}{\text{Line 2 + Line 3 Sessions (229)}} = 27.95\% \approx 28.00\%$.
